# Evaluator Instructions (Template — Specification Only)

> This is a specification template. It contains **no** human results and no fabricated
> labels. To be filled by the study administrator in a future authorized pilot task.

## Purpose
Score each candidate translation on 7 dimensions (7-point Likert) + binary ACCEPT/REJECT +
failure tags, using the S7-11 v1.1 rubric.

## Scale
1 = lowest quality … 7 = highest quality. Anchors per v1.1; do not redefine.

## Dimensions
Naturalness · Fidelity · Character Voice · Register/Tone · Coherence · Terminology ·
Added/Missing Detail.

## Binary
ACCEPT / REJECT — record separately from Likert; do not assume Likert implies the label.

## Failure Tags
Use the predefined v1.1 tags only. New needs → `PROPOSED_NEW_TAG` (no taxonomy change).

## Conduct
- Work independently; do not discuss items with other evaluators.
- You may abstain ("cannot judge"); abstention is recorded, not penalized.
- You may withdraw at any time.

# Training Protocol (Template — Specification Only)

No human results. Filled by the study administrator in a future authorized pilot task.

## Objective
Ensure the evaluator understands the rubric, the scale, the binary label, and the failure
tags before scoring pilot items.

## Steps (specification)
1. Orientation: read rubric + anchors.
2. Review annotated example items (disjoint from pilot/holdout).
3. Discuss boundaries and ambiguous cases.
4. Confirm instruction comprehension (qualification gate).

## Separation
Training items are disjoint from practice, pilot, calibration, and holdout sets.

## Evidence
Comprehension results are `qualification evidence` only — never literary-quality evidence.

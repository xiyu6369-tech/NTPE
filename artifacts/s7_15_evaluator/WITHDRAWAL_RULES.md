# Withdrawal Rules (Template — Specification Only)

## Allowed
An evaluator may withdraw at any time; no justification required.

## Handling
- Withdrawn responses retained as raw (never silently deleted).
- Partial data flagged; analysable only under pre-registered rules.
- Withdrawal recorded as a protocol event.

## Replacement
Replacement allowed only by a pre-registered rule; reason logged.

## Prohibited
No exclusion of an evaluator for low agreement, unfavorable ratings, or unexpected
preference.
